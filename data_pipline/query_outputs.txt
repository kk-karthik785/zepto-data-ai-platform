Q5_JOIN
============================================================
SQL Query:
SELECT
                b.title,
                b.price_gbp,
                b.rating,
                c.category_name
            FROM books AS b
            JOIN categories AS c
                ON b.category_id = c.category_id
            ORDER BY b.rating DESC
